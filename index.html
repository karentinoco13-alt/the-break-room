Customers have rated companies forever. Now it's the employees' turn. Rate your employer 1 to 5: the good, the bad and the worst. No sponsored badges. No paid awards. Just the people who clock in. Treat people well and they'll say so. If you don't, now you'll hear about it.

Your privacy comes first

This room only works if you can say it all. So it's built to keep you out of sight.

  • Your name is never shownNot on your rating, not anywhere. Ratings carry no name, email or photo.
  • Your employer can't see who wrote itNo company gets your identity, and no company can buy, hide or edit a rating.
  • Safety in numbersNothing about a company shows until 5 people rate it. Teams, locations and reasons for leaving need 5 too.
  • You share only what you chooseRole, team and comments are all optional. Skip anything that feels too close.
  • No ads, no tracking, never soldNo tracking cookies, no analytics, no data sales. Ever.
  • Delete it all, any timeEdit or delete your rating, or erase everything you've posted with one button.

So say it all, straight and honest. That's what this room is for.

One house rule: keep it professional. Proper manners and vocabulary, always. Profanity isn't accepted here, and ratings that use it can't be posted.

Why it's called The Break Room

In the break room, virtual or not, employees say how their employer really treats them. The ratings are savage. Nobody holds back. They talk about the manager who didn't stand up for them, and the tools that are clunky and useless, even though nobody said so during training.

In the break room, people get to be human, with all their imagination. They have the fix for that broken diagnostic tool they use every day, the one they're tired of raising with a manager who never does anything about it. They're over it. But in the break room there are no constraints. They rest, and they say how their jobs could be better and more efficient when no manager is listening. And honestly, even if one were, they wouldn't understand.

This is that room. Open to everyone, and off the record.

Our mission: open communication, unfiltered by management, for companies that want to do better. Honest feedback from the people doing the work, turned into better communication, process improvement and innovation.

One difference from the real break room: here you talk about the company, the team and the tools, never a person by name. That's what keeps you protected.

Terms of use

  • Free to use. The Break Room is free while it is being tested for feedback.
  • First-hand ratings only. Only current or former employees may rate an employer, and only based on their own experience.
  • No paid or fake ratings. No rewarded or pressured ratings. Employers and anyone acting for them may not rate their own company or a competitor.
  • Rate the company, not a person. Do not post names of coworkers or managers, contact details, or anything that identifies someone.
  • Professional decorum. Keep proper manners and vocabulary in everything you post. Profanity, insults and slurs are not accepted. The app blocks common profanity, and the developer removes anything that breaks this rule.
  • Keep it lawful. No threats, harassment, hate speech, statements you know are false, or confidential business information or trade secrets.
  • Opinions, not facts. Ratings are the opinions of the people who wrote them. They are not verified or endorsed by the developer, and this app is not affiliated with any employer listed.
  • Your responsibility. You are responsible for what you post. Any decision you make based on ratings here, such as taking or leaving a job, is yours.
  • Time at the company. Each rating shows how long the person says they worked there and whether they still do, so readers can weigh experience. This is self-reported and not verified. Giving false information breaks these terms.
  • Divisions and groups. You may name the division, team or location your rating is about and flag it as better or worse than the rest of the company. Name a group, never a person. A group's name and scores are shown only after 5 people from it have rated.
  • The full journey. You can rate the hiring process, onboarding and how your exit was handled, and say how it ended and why. Reasons for leaving appear only in company totals once 5 former employees answer, unless you choose to show your own story.
  • Readers weigh in. When someone shares how their job ended, readers can vote on who had room to do better: the employee, the employer, both or neither. Votes are anonymous, one per reader per story, and can be changed.
  • Minimum of 5 ratings. An employer's scores, award check and comments are shown only after 5 people have rated it, so nobody can work out who wrote what.
  • Moderation. One rating per employer per person. The developer may remove any rating that breaks these terms.

  • Changes. These terms may change as the app develops. Continuing to use the app means you accept the current version.

Privacy and security

  • What is stored. Your scores, comments, the role, division and employment status you enter, the date, and an anonymous account ID. Feedback you send and votes you cast are stored the same way.
  • Why the ID. It links a rating to your account so you can edit or delete it, and so each person rates an employer only once.
  • What is never stored. This app never saves or shows your name, email address or photo.
  • Who sees what. Everyone with access sees ratings and comments, with no names, and only for employers with at least 5 ratings. This is a display rule in the app, not encryption. The developer can see the anonymous ID but not your name. Your feedback goes only to the developer.
  • Where it lives. Data is stored by the Claude artifact service that hosts this app, so Anthropic's privacy policy also applies. Access is limited by sharing permissions and per-person write rules.
  • In this browser. The only thing saved on your device is that you accepted these terms.
  • No tracking. No ads, no tracking cookies, no analytics. Your data is never sold.
  • Your control. Use "Delete all my data" at the bottom of the page to erase your ratings and feedback. Deleting is permanent.
